# X tagging guide for the CmdWarden launch

Launch draft. Pair with `x-thread.md`.

## How to tag

1. Type `@` in the compose box. Then type the first letters of the name.
2. A list opens. Click the right account. X turns it into a blue link.
3. Check the account has the blue or gold check mark and the right name. Fake accounts copy names.
4. Post. The tagged account gets a notification.

## Rules that raise the reach

- Do not tag in post 1. X shows a first post with tags to fewer people.
- Tag in a reply to your own thread. One reply, two to four tags, one sentence.
- Tag people, not only companies. A person replies. A company account does not.
- Reply to their posts too. Find a post from them about agent safety or tokens. Reply with one line and the link.
- Space the tagged replies over two days. Not all in one hour.
